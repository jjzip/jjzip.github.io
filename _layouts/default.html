Jingjing Jiang

I am a second-year Ph.D. student at Nagoya University, advised by Prof. Ryuichiro Higashinaka and a member of the Dialogue System Research Group.

My research interests lie in the dynamics of face-to-face human interactions and the development of natural human-agent interaction. During my master’s, I collected and analyzed multimodal face-to-face dialogue data, with a broader focus on understanding the affective aspects of interaction. My current research focuses on real-time multimodal human-agent interaction, particularly integrating talking-head generation with full-duplex spoken dialogue systems.
