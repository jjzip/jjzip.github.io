I am Jingjing Jiang, a Ph.D. student in the [Dialogue System Research Group](https://www.ds.is.i.nagoya-u.ac.jp/) at Nagoya University, where I work on multimodal human-agent interaction. My current research focuses on **face-to-face full-duplex spoken dialogue systems**, in which the agent perceives the user's speech and facial behavior while concurrently generates its own, toward real-time, photorealistic human-agent interaction. During my master's, I developed a **physiological signal-based emotion estimation framework**, built on a multimodal human-human dialogue corpus collected by our group.
