I am a Ph.D. candidate in the [Dialogue System Research Group](https://www.ds.is.i.nagoya-u.ac.jp/) at Nagoya University, working on **human–computer interaction** and **multimodal spoken dialogue systems**. I want dialogue systems to communicate the way people do — reading and responding with voice, expression, and emotion in real time.

## Research interests

- Spoken & multimodal dialogue systems
- Full-duplex, real-time interaction
- Facial motion & audiovisual generation
- Affective computing & mental-state estimation
- Human–computer interaction
