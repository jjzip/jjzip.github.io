I am a Ph.D. candidate in the [Dialogue System Research Group](https://www.ds.is.i.nagoya-u.ac.jp/) at Nagoya University, working on **multimodal full-duplex dialogue systems** and **physiological signals based emotion estimation**. Now I am focusing on dialogue systems to communicate the way people do — reading and responding with voice, expression, and emotion in real time.

## Research interests

- Multimodal full-duplex dialogue systems
- Facial motion generation
- Physiological signals based emotion estimation
